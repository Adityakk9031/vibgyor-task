# Engineering Notes (`NOTES.md`)

## 1. How Plans, Walls, Rooms, and Amenities Were Extracted from the Drawing
- **Units, Block Expansion & Plan Alignment**: `$INSUNITS` in `SUBMISSION - 04.dxf` is unset (`0`), and dimension callouts verify `1 DXF unit = 1.0 metre` (building footprint $13.12\text{ m} \times 38.733\text{ m}$, net built-up area $398.59\text{ m}^2$). Expanding all `INSERT` block references recursively via `ezdxf.virtual_entities()` (including `A$C27EF3DA3` for RERA flats and `A$C71DB458C` for ground stilt/lobby) and anchoring each plan's SW `A_BUILT UP` corner to `(0, 0)` aligns the **Basement Plan**, **Ground (Stilt & Lobby) Plan**, **1st–7th Typical Floor Plan**, **Terrace Plan**, and **Site Layout Plan** into a single building-relative metre coordinate frame.
- **Wall-Snapped Room Extraction & Semantic Tagging (`extract_structured_rooms`)**: On the typical floor, room callout `MTEXT` entities on `A_TEXT` (`"DRAWING ROOM 6.53 x 3.65"`, `"KITCHEN/DINING 6.59 x 3.20"`, `"BED ROOM 3.96 x 5.02"`, etc.) provide the room name, callout dimensions $(w, d)$, and label anchor $(x_r, y_r)$. `pipeline/extract.py` snaps each room's $[x_0, y_0, x_1, y_1]$ bounding box to the nearest parallel inner wall edges on `g-wall`/`A_Wall` whose span matches $(w, d)$ within $0.35\text{ m}$, classifies the room into `type_a` ($y < 19.1\text{ m}$) or `type_b` ($y > 22.9\text{ m}$), and intersects a $0.32\text{ m}$ boundary buffer with extracted windows (`A_WINDOW`), sliding doors (`g-dw`), and hinged doors (`A_DOOR`) to tag which wall (`north`, `south`, `east`, `west`) contains each opening.

## 2. How Furniture and Amenity Layouts Are Generated from Room Geometry (`pipeline/furnish.py`)
- **Zero Hand-Typed Furniture Coordinates**: Every room in both **Type A (Front 4BHK, $160.05\text{ m}^2$)** and **Type B (Rear 4BHK, $143.18\text{ m}^2$)** is furnished procedurally from its extracted `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` room bounds and wall opening tags:
  - **Drawing Rooms**: Detects the balcony sliding-door wall (`east` in Type A, `west` in Type B) and places the L-shaped sectional sofa against the primary solid wall (`north` in Type A, `south` in Type B), nested marble/brass coffee tables at the seating centroid, fluted wood TV console opposite the sofa, perimeter recessed LED cove ceiling, and sheer curtains flanking the sliding doors.
  - **Kitchen & Dining**: Places the L-shaped quartz counter, base/overhead cabinetry, hob, and breakfast island with 2 coral barstools on the utility side (`west` in Type A, `east` in Type B) and centers the 6-seater dining table, upholstered chairs, and linear brass pendant light in the dining half.
  - **Bedrooms (Master & Second/Third/Fourth)**: Selects the primary solid headboard wall from the room bounds and places the king/queen bed, upholstered headboard, fluted feature panel, pillows, throw blanket, twin floating nightstands with warm lamps, and a full-height sliding wardrobe along the adjacent solid wall.
- **Amenity Spaces**: Basement and Ground stilt car bays (`A_CAR`) and two-wheeler bays (`A_SCOOTER`) are extruded directly from the DXF rectangles with yellow bay markings and populated with procedural 3D vehicles oriented along each bay's principal axis. The Ground Entrance Lobby (`[4.265, 19.094, 8.965, 23.084]`) is fitted with travertine marble, fluted wood panelling, 2 bronze lift doors, a glowing ring chandelier, and lounge seating.

## 3. How the 16 $360^\circ$ Viewpoints Were Chosen
- All 16 equirectangular cameras (`PANORAMA_SHOTS` in `pipeline/render.py`) sit at standing eye height ($1.50\text{ m}$ above the local floor slab) with a strictly level horizon (`rotation_euler = (pi/2, 0, yaw)`) and are verified by a 3D raycast audit to have $>0.70\text{ m}$ clearance from all walls, columns, and furniture:
  - **Building & Amenities (6)**: (1) Entrance Gate & Canopy Drive, (2) Ground Entrance Lobby & Lifts, (3) Basement Parking & Curved Ramp, (4) Landscape Garden Deck & Stilt Parking, (5) Terrace Pergola Lounge & Kids' Play Area, and (6) Terrace Fitness Gym & Yoga Deck.
  - **Type A Interior (5)** & **Type B Interior (5)**: Placed at the open walk-through centroids of (1) Drawing Room, (2) Kitchen & Dining, (3) Master Bedroom, (4) Second Bedroom, and (5) Third Bedroom / Balcony View in each flat so 3D hotspots connect naturally along the circulation path.

## 4. Render Settings, Times & Terrace Assumptions
- **Render Settings & Times**: Rendered headlessly with **Blender Cycles** (`AgX - Medium High Contrast` tone mapping, OpenImageDenoise enabled, adaptive sampling threshold $0.08$, 3 max light bounces, CC0 Poly Haven HDRIs + balanced warm interior point lights so windows never blow out). On CPU, each $2560 \times 1440$ still takes $\sim 2.5\text{--}3.5\text{ min}$ ($8\text{--}10$ adaptive samples) and each $6144 \times 3072$ equirectangular panorama takes $\sim 3.5\text{--}4.5\text{ min}$ ($6$ adaptive samples).
- **Assumptions**: Because the municipal `OPEN TERRACE PLAN` in `SUBMISSION - 04.dxf` shows only the outer parapet (`A_PARAPET`), central stair/lift cabin (`A_Wall`), O.H.W.T., and solar PV note, we partitioned the open terrace slab ($z = 24.70\text{ m}$) into three non-overlapping zones matching the developer brochure (`brochure/12.jpg`, `15.jpg`, `16.jpg`, `20.jpg`): a **South-Front Kids' Play Area** ($y \in [0.8, 9.8]\text{ m}$), a **South-Central Slatted Pergola Lounge** ($y \in [10.4, 18.2]\text{ m}$), and a **North Glass Fitness Gym Pavilion with Solar Roof & West Yoga Deck** ($y \in [24.2, 37.6]\text{ m}$).
